🦆A quacky classic

Duck in a Tux

A duck wore socks and a shiny tux,
Then rode to town in a carrot truck.
“I’m off to dance!” he yelled with glee—
And moonwalked straight into a pea!

🐉A snack-time saga

Dragon’s Toast

A dragon made toast with a sneeze and a puff,
It flew past the moon—now that’s high enough!
He buttered the stars and munched with a grin,
Then brushed every tooth with a cinnamon spin.

🐸A pond-side bop

Frog’s New Shoes

A frog got boots that went squeak, squeak, squeak,
He tap-danced softly for a whole frog week.
The fish clapped fins, the turtles cheered,
Then everyone’s toes disappeared!
